Audience notes: $ARGUMENTS

Draft a 3 to 5 minute demo pitch for a non-technical decision maker. Use short, speakable sentences and no jargon.
1. The client's problem, and what we built in one sentence.
2. A demo script of 3 to 4 clicks.
3. How it works in plain language, and where the AI fits.
4. Honest limitations.
5. Next steps, and what production would take.
6. 4 or 5 likely client questions (cost, data privacy, accuracy, scale, timeline), each with a 1 to 2 sentence answer.
